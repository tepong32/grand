How to maintain a statement mapping

  1. Compare the latest signed local statement and current COA/GAM guidance with the starter.
  2. Create a successor and keep line labels readable for Accounting staff.
  3. Assign every active posting account exactly once; correct every coverage warning.
  4. Record the reviewed authority and local acceptance evidence.
  5. Submit. A different authorized reviewer activates the version.
  6. Generate both management statements and inspect equation, coverage, freshness, and JEV drill-through before relying on them.

Closing a period does not mean the statements were accepted. Statement review and official-form fidelity remain separate controlled decisions.
