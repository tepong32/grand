How to close an accounting period

  1. Finish, post, return, or discard every JEV in the period.
  2. Review adjusting entries and, at year-end, applicable closing entries. Record what was checked even when no entry is needed.
  3. Prepare the checklist. GRAND reads the current trial balance and the locally governed subsidiary, bank, statement, and handoff evidence.
  4. Resolve red required gates. Yellow warnings under the starter policy are visible for local confirmation but do not silently claim official acceptance.
  5. Submit to a different authorized reviewer. Approval closes the period and blocks new posting.
  6. If a correction is later required, record the reason and retained authority. A different reviewer decides the reopen; later periods must be reopened first.
