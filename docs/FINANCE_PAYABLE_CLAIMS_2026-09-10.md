## Operator behavior

Accounting can identify a newly recognized claim on its liability credit with a stable payee key and invoice/claim reference. A later liability debit selects that posted original credit. Separate debit lines can apply to separate claims, even when they share a payee. The original expense is not recorded again by this manual settlement JEV.

The journal detail shows the claim and links applications back to their source JEV. The existing subsidiary-control screen now includes individual claims, original recognized amounts, applied amounts and outstanding balances at the selected date. Its archived CSV retains fund, control account, payee, claim, source JEV/line and amounts. The existing payee/control-account schedule remains available and includes the automatically created immutable subsidiary details.

## Financial controls and persistence

- `accounting/0016_individual_payable_claims.py` adds party/claim text and a protected self-reference on `JournalLine` in the Finance store. Legacy fields remain blank; no old journal is relabelled. No second ledger, mutable outstanding-balance registry or cross-database foreign key is introduced.
- The original claim must be a posted, attributed liability credit. An application uses the same office, fund and control account and cannot predate recognition. Independent existing submission/posting rules remain in force.
- Posting locks original credit lines in ascending ID order before checking applications. It checks the cumulative amount at every affected accounting date, not merely today's available balance. Later restoration cannot justify a backdated overpayment.
- Claim restoration is allowed only through an actual, exactly mirrored reversal of a posted application. Reversing an original recognition also consumes that claim's capacity; it cannot erase a liability while leaving its settlement in place. Failed posting remains submitted and rolls back newly created subsidiary evidence.
- New claim identities are checked under the existing fund row lock. The same payee/claim reference in one fund cannot be recognized again, including casing variants tested on SQLite/MySQL. A posted original keeps that identity even after reversal; an independently reviewed correction needs a distinct correction reference and retained reversal evidence. This is a posting-service guarantee, not a claim of a new database-wide uniqueness constraint.
- Statement source snapshots retain nonblank claim/reference links. Old blank-source snapshots keep their existing shape. Archived claim CSVs preserve actual generated bytes under existing export controls.
