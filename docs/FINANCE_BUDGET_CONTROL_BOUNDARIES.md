## Source and authority constraints

Whole Form 1 office lookup rows are candidate indexes, never access authority.
Register counts and pages require original package/source viewing checks before
pagination; exports recheck separate original export rights. Retain original
history and unknown controls. Migration 0022 preflights root/office evidence rather
than repairing history. See [review and delivery contract](FINANCE_FORM1_PACKAGE_REVIEW_DESIGN.md).

Form 1 receipt forecasts must partition every retained planning source explicitly,
map included amounts to receipt leaves/accounts and retain exact overlap anchors.
Copied resource identities retain their original amount cap; matching labels do not
prove duplication. Zero estimates differ from missing estimates. Pure arithmetic
never supplies Budget Officer review, joint actuals, cash capacity or form issuance;
see the [source contract](FINANCE_FORM1_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md).
Retained receipt forecast decisions cover the whole proposal/Fund entity inventory,
share proposal-before-Fund version/source locks and require explicit independent
Budget Officer authority. Source caps and funding identities apply across entities;
pending successors block older new approval. Historical review and currentness stay
separate. Forecast review is neither statutory joint actuals nor Form 1 issuance.
Guided forecast intake starts unresolved, preserves every source while paging and
requires explicit partitions, overlap counterparts and category declarations.
Signed previews are actor/source/version bound; complete exports separately check
Budget and original native export authority, including authorized export-only users.
Form 1 assembly must retain explicit historical leaf coverage, original account
itemization and cross-entity source/office allocation. Category totals and entity
labels do not prove those boundaries. Future opening cash requires a forecast
bridge; preserve stock dates and explicit PhilHealth overlap evidence. See the
[assembly design](FINANCE_FORM1_ASSEMBLY_DESIGN.md).
Cash stock selection reuses adopted historical or jointly certified native sources,
matching all three offices, Fund/entity and exact date/boundary. New selection
requires current source evidence; retained history rechecks access without replacing
original controls. A cash source is neither a forecast bridge nor spending capacity.
Cash forecast drafts retain original authorized actual anchors and a separate cash
timing worksheet. Explicit projected cash movements and dated restriction changes
must reproduce the target boundary; missing coverage remains unknown. Day-end
restriction reconciliation never establishes intra-day capacity or officer approval.
Opening-stock drafts retain an explicit annual-boundary convention: current annual
opening references H1 January cash, never the sum of January and July stocks.
Draft resource ending arithmetic is distinct from certified actual closing cash.
Governed retention/review and complete stock/PhilHealth assembly remain open.
PhilHealth deduction treatment retains original receipt, opening restriction and
expense identities/periods. Health labels do not prove SHF funding; ordinary LGU
counterpart expenses and component-LGU Trust Fund transfers remain distinct.
Unresolved overlap cannot silently reduce deductions or create cash capacity; see
the [source audit](FINANCE_FORM1_PHILHEALTH_SOURCE_AUDIT.md).
Period-flow composition retains missing historical leaves as unknown and original
source controls separately. Reject repeated whole native Fund/period use across
entities; shared-source row allocation needs its own reviewed evidence. Flow
coverage alone does not supply stock, official account itemization or issuance.
Original Form 1 component inventory retains financial-line/partition evidence and
signed amounts. Receipt cash accounts are source accounts, not inferred reporting
accounts. Current totals are derived, never another source cohort; see the source
contract for itemization scope and validation.
Reporting-account drafts retain the original ledger account separately from each
explicit reporting selection. Partition signed amounts exactly; never use a local
account code as a government code. Captured reporting dates must cover the exact
native date or whole undated source interval. Retirement does not erase actual
history, while forecast selections require active posting accounts. Draft selections
do not establish government-code authority or independent Accounting approval.
Retained Form 1 account review is a separate Budget preparation/Accounting decision
over the original full component package and code-authority attachment. Dedicated
package access is scoped to its Budget office and original Accounting Fund owner;
it never grants Accounting general Budget editing. Recheck original source access
on reads/exports and source freshness before new approval. Missing actual coverage,
joint certification, cash evidence and form issuance remain separate boundaries.
Guided account intake preserves all original components across search/paging,
starts unresolved and requires explicit signed splits and code-authority evidence.
Actor/source/version-bound previews reject stale preparation. Registers count only
source-authorized records; full JSON and original attachment downloads recheck
separate source export authority. Browser proof is synthetic, not LGU acceptance.
Itemized Form 1 output must preserve signed source shares, missing category coverage
and real net-zero activity. Derive receipt hierarchy subtotals from unique leaves;
never add account rows and their parent totals together. SPA class/account/program
labels do not establish Annex D subdivision attribution; require separate reviewed
source treatment before complete assembly. See the assembly design's source audit.
Printable flow drafts retain the complete reviewed package and source-share index;
recheck dedicated and original-source export access before PDF/JSON delivery.
Original approvals remain distinct from freshness and final issuance. Every printed
page must identify an incomplete draft; unsupported source glyphs fail closed.
See the source contract for deterministic-byte and browser validation.
Detached itemized-flow envelopes preserve original account/share evidence but do
not inherit Accounting approval or source access; delivery must check those separately.
SPA subdivision drafts partition original reporting-account shares with unchanged
signs, components and source bodies. Require explicit coverage for every entity,
source column and named subdivision; missing original SPA coverage stays unknown.
Net-zero class totals do not erase opposing subdivision activity. Pure partitions
do not supply Budget/Accounting approval or legal spending authorization; see the
assembly design and source contract for retention and certification boundaries.
Retained SPA versions pin the original approved account decision and authority;
separate immutable Budget/Accounting decisions require distinct actors and explicit
source/subdivision verification. Proposal-before-Fund-before-SPA locks and
unconditional version/role identities serialize decisions on native MySQL. Returns
close versions while retaining prior approvals. Original source read/export access
and latest source/account/SPA versions remain independent of historical approval.
Combined flow/SPA drafts must reproduce the exact original approved account source
and retained SPA decisions, all five amount columns, signed parts and coverage.
Class/subdivision/account controls describe the same money. Pure checksums never
authorize attachments or source access; use the existing source service before
delivery. Historical draft output grants no cash, currentness or final issuance.
SPA review does not grant spending authority or final Form 1 certification.
Whole Form 1 assembly requires exact equality of the complete account/SPA and
stock/PhilHealth flow sources, with every entity, scope, row and five-column
control retained. Arithmetic coverage never inherits whole-package approval,
source freshness, cash capacity or issuance. Current source selection and retained
history/export remain distinct; existing Budget-owned forecast access is not
automatic Accounting/Treasury whole-package authorization. See the assembly design.
Retained whole-form preparations pin original SPA/account/forecast models and
cash/PhilHealth attachment bytes. Unconditional version/attachment identities and
proposal-before-Fund locks govern new preparation. Dedicated package roles and
original source read/export permissions allow only the original Budget/Accounting/
Treasury package scope; they never grant generic Budget editing or final review,
certification, issuance or cash authority. Preserve prior versions and bytes.
Whole-package source currentness is separate from completeness and retained approval.
Use the acting office's original permissions; never borrow the maker's identity.
New decisions must observe sources inside their owning proposal-before-Fund
transaction; a standalone freshness result does not freeze ledger posting or
authorize later issuance. See the [review/delivery contract](FINANCE_FORM1_PACKAGE_REVIEW_DESIGN.md).
Internal whole-package decisions retain distinct actors and explicit role/office
checks under proposal-before-Fund-before-package locks. Derive state from immutable
rows; returns close versions without rewriting preparation or earlier approvals.
Complete current controls are mandatory for new approval; pending overlap/SHF or
missing evidence stays unresolved. Internal review is separate from statutory
certifiers, Chief Executive approval, issuance and cash capacity.

Budget office reconciliation may select one jointly certified native or adopted
historical actuals source per exact comparison period. Native source selection
retains the complete joint snapshot and trial balance; aggregate category rows do
not invent account attribution. Schema-3 acceptance rechecks source currentness
and latest issued office versions under proposal-before-Fund locks. Existing
schema-1/2 history stays unchanged. Reconciliation does not issue Form 1; see the
[source contract](FINANCE_FORM1_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md).
Retained native reconciliation detail/review requires current native source read
authority; complete exports separately require native source export authority.

Native Budget receipt inventory retains a complete ledger cohort and independently
reviewed cash scope, reproduces governed collection source rows, and preserves
unresolved classifications. Cash debit, credit and net movement are audit controls,
never receipt totals; empty periods do not certify zero receipts. Source currentness
is separate from detached integrity. Do not infer receipt categories from account
titles, collection kind or cash-flow purpose. See the
[Form 1 source contract](FINANCE_FORM1_SOURCE_CONTRACT.md).
Receipt partition proposals cover every line and preserve explicit exclusions;
exact reversals inherit complete predecessor components through bijective financial
line pairing. Earlier originals remain dependencies, never extra period activity.
Proposal arithmetic does not supply review, certified zero or joint actuals authority.
Local receipt attribution review retains complete batches, immutable member versions
and exact predecessor review snapshots. Fund-first decisions require independent
reviewers and current source/version pins; returns preserve evidence. Dedicated
receipt permissions/UAT denial apply. Reviewed classification is separate from joint
certification, reporting-entity coverage and Form 1 issuance; see the source contract.
Guided receipt classification retains explicit split components and exact original-line
pairing for reversals. Original source descriptions/references support retrieval only;
they never infer category or exclusion. Filtered views retain complete batch decisions
and exports; unresolved defaults require evidence before any independent review.
Receipt report cohorts capture current heads under that Fund boundary and retain
complete period lines/predecessor decisions. Missing, newer or stale reviews remain
unresolved; earlier originals are not extra activity. Diagnostic category arithmetic
does not establish category/entity coverage or statutory joint certification. Ordinary
post-read posting can make the returned coherent cohort stale; retain it unchanged.
Exact-period ledger views must derive from the same retained capture, preserve
reversal/claim dependency closure and exclude earlier sources from activity. Keep
the parent evidence; detached checksums do not grant authority. Existing expenditure
class labels are free text: do not infer Form 1 category mappings from those labels.
Native actuals preparation reproduces receipts and expenditures from one retained
ledger observation and requires both read capabilities. New receipt partition
schema 2 excludes only the separate expenditure attribution head from its source
fingerprint; schema 1 remains reproducible with its original broader fingerprint.
Complete category/entity declarations and class mappings are proposals, not
independent review, statutory certification or issued Form 1 authority.
Native actuals category/entity preparations now retain complete year/H1 evidence,
immutable versions and independent Accounting review observations under Fund-first

Dedicated permissions/UAT mutation denial apply. This review does not replace
named Accountant/Treasurer joint certification or authorize financial posting.
Native joint actuals decisions require separately authorized designated Accountant
and Treasurer actors, distinct from the preparer and each other. They pin reviewed
category/entity versions and same-observation Fund trial balances; returns close
remaining roles without inventing observation. Partner retained view/export is
explicitly scoped; it does not grant general live-ledger access. Freshness remains
separate from immutable historical certification and ordinary posting.
Guided actuals require explicit all-category/entity confirmation and expense-class
mappings; missing classifications or mappings never become certified zeros.
Display paging/filtering cannot truncate retained source/decision/TB exports.
Dedicated role definitions do not authorize operator provisioning or Form 1 issuance.
