## Independent review responsibilities

Budget reviews the complete entity inventory, approved estimates, stock-boundary
convention, projected cash timing and planning-resource reconciliation. Accounting
reviews original ledger/adopted actuals, reporting-account/SPA evidence, source
controls and proposed PhilHealth treatment. Treasury reviews original actual cash,
restriction identities, deposit/collection lineage and cash-timing evidence within
its designated source scope. A policy affecting more than one responsibility needs
the corresponding independent decisions, not a free-text approval by one officer.

Retain explicit designation authority, actual actor/department, timestamp, package
checksum, reviewed source observation, decision and note. Makers cannot review their
own package; one actor cannot fill multiple required independent roles. UAT denies
decisions despite additional operational grants. Viewing/exporting stays separately
authorized against the actual original models and files for the acting officer.
Never substitute the maker's account to make another officer's freshness check pass.

If members name different Treasury offices, derive required reviews from their
original scopes. A decision for one office must identify exactly its covered members
and cannot certify another office's cash. Test this topology before supporting it;
current whole-package source access is not proof of that additional workflow.

## Currentness and immutable decisions

Currentness is an authorized observation, distinct from arithmetic completeness,
historical approval and issuance. Check the latest whole preparation and original
SPA/account/forecast heads, exact reconciliation/resource/account captures, actuals
cohorts, issued office versions and actual/forecast cash anchors. A pending successor
blocks a new approval of the older source, even if its earlier approval remains valid
historically. Missing coverage can be current while remaining incomplete.

Acquire proposal, Fund and review identity in that order before approval checks and
writes. Retain the exact observation in each decision. Use unconditional native
identities for version and role/office uniqueness; do not depend on SQLite-only
conditional uniqueness. A standalone `is_current` result is never a token that
authorizes a later decision: recheck within the owning decision transaction. Fund
locks do not freeze ordinary ledger posting. Preserve observed cohort evidence and
recheck before subsequent certification/issuance rather than claiming a global freeze.

Derive review status from immutable decision rows. A return closes that version and
retains prior decisions. It can describe stale but accessible original evidence;
new approval requires the current complete source. Never overwrite the prepared
payload or an earlier approval to repair a treatment. A corrected preparation pins
its predecessor and receives fresh independent decisions.
