## Why this is the next functional phase

The original [modernization priorities](FINANCE_MODERNIZATION_PRIORITIES.md) identify collection/deposit and liquidation as M03 parity gaps. The retained [eGAPS observation](EGAPS_GRAND_PLAN.md#functional-shape) confirms the accounting menu paths; it does not prove detailed source-entry behavior or locally used receipt types. GRAND's collection/deposit source workflow remains missing after the recent M02 payable/remittance work. Further remittance fees and agency-disposition extensions must not indefinitely displace this confirmed domain gap.

This reconciles execution with the existing user direction: complete missing daily Finance functions before expanding surrounding machinery. Fees/netted receipts, outgoing agency repayments, non-cash dispositions and generated-history adoption remain unfinished; they are not silently removed from the objective.

## First complete office scenario to implement

Implement the cash-collection-to-bank-deposit chain together, rather than representing a new menu or generic journal form as collection coverage:

1. An authorized Treasury collector records an actual receipt with its controlled book/series and number, date, payer/reference, fund, collection classification, amount and supporting evidence. Retain the collector and Treasury office. Duplicate actual receipt identity must be refused even under concurrent entry.
2. A reviewed Finance posting recipe supplies the actual collection treatment. Preserve its version and account evidence; do not infer a revenue account, tax rate, fund or authority from labels. Accounting prepares the corresponding JEV and independent posting establishes the collected amount in the ledger.
3. Treasury selects explicit posted receipt amounts for an actual deposit, retaining bank, deposit date/slip and each allocation. A 100 collection supports a 60 deposit followed by 40; 41, duplicate allocation, another office/fund or a date before collection must be refused. Pending deposits reserve capacity once. Actual bank fees, bounced checks and other different events must not be disguised as deposit amounts.
4. Independent Accounting posting transfers the allocated amount from collection cash to the actual bank. Depositing money must not recognize the same revenue again. Preserve internal cash-transfer meaning for cash-flow reporting.
5. The collection/deposit register and exports reproduce receipt amount, deposited amount, remaining undeposited amount, collector, deposit slip and linked JEVs. Replay the ledger/control and output totals, including partial deposits, instead of merely checking that records exist.
6. Complete rejection/correction, discarded-draft recovery and posted reversal/source-allocation handling as part of the same scenario. Posted source history cannot be overwritten; a failed cross-store handoff cannot duplicate receipt or deposit posting or release capacity prematurely.

Use existing Finance setup, journal posting, account permissions, audit, export and separate-store contracts. Do not create a competing general-purpose workflow or configuration engine. Decide the smallest source-record structure that can support the entire receipt/deposit chain before adding it; a JournalEntry alone cannot substitute for the missing collector/deposit source controls.
