# GRAND Finance field-acceptance starter pack

Status: editable planning and evidence aid for the F10–F11 field-acceptance train. It is not an approved LGU form set, an authority to use any national form, a substitute for GRAND's governed records, or a cutover authorization.

## Starter workbook

Open the [GRAND Finance Field Acceptance Starter](../../outputs/grand-finance-field-acceptance/GRAND_Finance_Field_Acceptance_Starter.xlsx). It is intentionally conservative, macro-free, and editable by ordinary spreadsheet users.

The workbook gives Budget, Accounting, Treasury, requesting offices, reviewers, and recovery staff one shared working file for:

- the LBP, LBA, LBR, LBE, and LBAc form families identified in the DBM Budget Operations Manual for LGUs, 2023 Edition;
- verified printed-manual/PDF page anchors for all 31 listed forms and 70 candidate field/control mappings spanning every listed form;
- transaction-specific COA documentary-rule intake without inventing unverified requirements;
- the seven F10.2 practical-test categories;
- office owners, different witnesses, exercises, field cycles, decisions, and evidence references;
- exact accepted-form set and checksum planning for F11.6;
- the structured two-store recovery-rehearsal inputs and RPO/RTO checks used by F11.7; and
- formula-driven planning counts that begin at zero accepted items.

Yellow cells with blue text are intended for staff input. Formula results remain black. Green status cells indicate that the workbook's own stated condition is met; they do not confer legal authority or replace independent acceptance in GRAND.

## Recommended working sequence

1. Enter the LGU, fiscal year, preparer, reviewer, and evidence-custody context on **Start Here**.
2. Review **Sources and Scope** and create a local decision for each applicability question.
3. In **Official Form Register**, confirm the exact current form name, revision/effectivity, local authority, delivery mode, owner, and differences from the starter.
4. Use the printed-manual/PDF anchors in **Official Form Register** to review the official source, then compare the retained blank or safely redacted local form with **Field Mapping**. Its 70 source-anchored starter rows cover all listed forms, but staff must still add or revise every locally different required, optional, conditional, or repeating section.
5. Populate **COA Document Rules** only from the exact current circular annex/section and locally approved transaction route. Keep uncertain entries in `To be extracted/confirmed` status.
6. Use a redacted or synthetic packet to perform all seven **Practical Tests** on the actual device, printer, paper, and form stock. The owner and witness must be different people.
7. Complete the role and non-functional **Exercises and Owners** plan, including accessibility, printing, support, succession, continuity, and recovery.
8. Run the locally approved number of consecutive **Field Cycles** and reconcile source-versus-GRAND counts and amounts by case, batch, period, register, ledger, and report.
9. Perform the **Recovery Rehearsal** in an isolated environment and retain the exact two-store backup identities, hashes, RPO/RTO timestamps, control results, cross-store case, runtime-file checks, disposal, and independent witness evidence.
10. Resolve every item on **Decisions** and review **Acceptance Summary**. In GRAND, open **Local form acceptance → Use DBM starter** for an applicable listed form, create its editable candidate, transfer and locally confirm the compared section decisions, then complete the existing source, reference, test, and independent-acceptance gates. Submit the resulting accepted-form set and field evidence through the corresponding F10/F11 screens.

## Official-source boundary

The starter uses these discovery references:

- [DBM Local Budget Circular No. 152 / Budget Operations Manual for LGUs, 2023 Edition](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/Issuances/2023/Local-Budget-Circular/Budget%20Operations%20Manual%20for%20LGUs%2C%202023%20Edition.pdf)
- [COA Circular No. 2023-004 — Updated Documentary Requirements for Common Government Transactions](https://www.coa.gov.ph/wpfd_file/coa-circular-no-2023-004-june-14-2023/)
- [COA Circular No. 92-389 — Disbursement Voucher, General Form No. 5A](https://www.coa.gov.ph/wpfd_file/coa-circular-no-92-389-november-3-1992/)

An official source still requires confirmation of current amendments, local applicability, LGU level, fund, fiscal year, transaction variant, signatory route, deadlines, and approved local copies. The workbook therefore uses `Candidate - confirm locally` and `To be extracted/confirmed` defaults rather than pre-approving the source.

## Relationship to GRAND

The workbook prepares content and evidence for existing controls; it does not duplicate them:

- [Finance local-form inventory and acceptance](../FINANCE_LOCAL_FORM_ACCEPTANCE.md) now includes an in-app catalog for all 31 source-anchored DBM candidates. Its 77 application sections are practical form groups derived from the workbook's 70 field/control mapping rows; both remain editable and locally unconfirmed until staff compare them with the actual form. GRAND remains the F10.2 register, practical-test, independent-acceptance, successor, and immutable-evidence workflow.
- [Finance shadow operation, UAT acceptance, and controlled cutover](../FINANCE_SHADOW_CUTOVER.md) remains the F11 exercise, field-cycle, accepted-form lineage, recovery, stakeholder decision, cutover, and rollback workflow.
- [Portable GRAND export archive](../EXPORT_ARCHIVE.md) remains the TraceSync-ready export boundary. The workbook should retain references and hashes instead of copying confidential packets or signature images into source control.
- [Database backup and recovery](../DATABASE_BACKUP.md) remains the authoritative two-store backup, verification, and isolated-restore operating contract.

## What must still come from the LGU

- Exact current blank or safely redacted local forms and continuation pages
- The current authority, ordinance, circular, memorandum, or written local decision for each enabled scope
- Named form owners, preparers, reviewers, certifiers, approvers, signatories, recipients, and evidence custodians
- Actual printer/device/paper/form-stock trials and accepted samples
- Current COA documentary requirements by transaction variant and any authorized exception route
- Local reporting cadence, package contents, recipients, deadlines, retention, and export/off-host custody route
- Locally approved field-cycle count, pass conditions, RPO/RTO, rollback trigger, and named cutover authority
- Independent acceptance recorded in GRAND

Until those items exist, the workbook remains a candidate starter and the parent F10/F11 gates remain open.
