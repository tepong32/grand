|---|---|---|

| `vouchers/collections.py`, `bank_collections.py` | Deposits reuse existing receipt allocations; direct-bank receipts follow a separate reviewed route | Receipt plus deposit is not two receipts; bank-account choice does not establish a revenue category |

| `vouchers/cheque_redemptions.py` | A replacement principal receipt settles a linked return; refund identity can survive replacement | A new instrument is not automatically new revenue; require original/return/redemption lineage |
| `accounting/budget_actuals.py` and `BudgetActualScope` | Existing external certificates are center/office expenditure evidence | Do not repurpose their Accountant-only authority or center uniqueness as fund-wide joint receipt certification |
| `accounting/budget_native_sources.py::_capture` | Captures fund ledger lines, posted evidence, reviewed expenditure and reversal/dependency context | Reuse coherent-capture principles, but its expenditure projection and period subset do not provide a certified opening cash balance |

These distinctions are visible in source, not assumptions about local eGAPS usage.
