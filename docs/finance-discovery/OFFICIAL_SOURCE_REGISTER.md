# Initial COA/DBM official-source and template register

Status: public-source baseline reviewed 2026-08-31. This is a routing register, not a legal opinion or proof of local applicability. Each source remains subject to effectivity, amendment, LGU-level, transaction, funding-source, and local-auditor/process-owner confirmation before GRAND enables official use.

The canonical rule is: **official reference**, **observed eGAPS behavior**, **LGU-confirmed practice**, and **GRAND-implemented behavior** are recorded separately. A familiar screen or old blank form never overrides a current issuance.

## Template intake rule

For every required form or report, create one controlled template record with:

- source ID, issuance/circular, appendix/form number, title, revision/effectivity, LGU level, and enabled transaction/fund scope;
- blank official or locally approved specimen reference and an access-controlled redacted completed sample;
- field map, code lists, repeating rows, totals/subtotals, cross-foot rules, certifications, wet-signature roles, copy count, print area, and submission destination;
- owner, reviewer, approver, superseded template, workbook/document checksum, mapping checksum, synthetic preview, and comparison result;
- unresolved applicability questions and the office/auditor authorized to answer them.

No PDF or spreadsheet from an official site becomes executable merely because it was downloaded. GRAND uses a reviewed native mapping or a macro-free, preflighted template version. Generated output remains non-official until its process-owner acceptance and the parent roadmap gate pass.

The F2.2 generic CSV and its controlled export are staging/data-interchange formats, not prescribed COA schedules. They require a reviewed mapping to the LGU's accepted chart, subsidiary detail, fund treatment, and opening/conversion evidence before production use.
