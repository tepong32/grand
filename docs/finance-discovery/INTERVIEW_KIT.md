# Finance office interview kit

Use one interview record per office, role separation, and transaction variant. Group sessions are useful for resolving handoffs, but individual role demonstrations are still required where maker-checker or custody separation matters.

## Before the session

- Confirm read-only eGAPS authority and the actions that are prohibited.
- Select the fiscal year, transaction type, and completed/redacted case; do not browse opportunistically through production records.
- Ask the evidence custodian to prepare approved blank or redacted artifacts and control totals.
- Prepare the transaction catalog row and anticipated evidence IDs.
- Assign a facilitator, actual-step recorder, balance/control-total recorder, and evidence custodian.
- Confirm that signatures, identifiers, accounts, checks, credentials, contacts, and infrastructure details will not enter the repository.

## Demonstration prompts

Ask the participant to demonstrate with approved evidence rather than describe an ideal route.

1. What authority, balance, paper, or system state arrived, and from which office/role?
2. What was verified before accepting custody or acting?
3. What was entered in eGAPS, GRAND, a spreadsheet, a paper register, or another system?
4. Which amount changed: proposal, appropriation, allotment, obligation, payable, ledger, deduction, or cash?
5. What number, form, stamp, checklist, printout, entry, register, or report resulted?
6. What approval or wet signature was required, under which authority, and in which order?
7. Who held the physical packet before and after, and how was receipt acknowledged?
8. What exact evidence allowed the next office to act?
9. What causes return, rejection, adjustment, reprint, reversal, cancellation, replacement, or reopening?
10. Which IDs, versions, balances, and documents survive a correction?
11. Which register, ledger, reconciliation, or report proves the period-end result?

For every answer, ask: “Which evidence ID demonstrates that?” If none exists, record an Unresolved step or decision rather than an LGU-confirmed rule.

## Cycle-specific probes

### Budget authority

- What versions exist from call and department proposal through ordinance and review?
- What exact event and evidence make an amount an approved appropriation?
- How are supplemental, reenacted, continuing, reserve, and conditionally approved amounts treated?
- How do approved schedules reconcile to operational opening balances?

### Allotment and obligation

- Which release, reserve, deferral, and adjustment instruments apply locally?
- When is an ALOBS/ORS/OBR number assigned and when is authority consumed?
- What equation produces unreleased appropriation and unobligated allotment?
- What happens when two requests compete for the same balance?

### Payable, DV, and paper route

- Which source documents establish delivery, acceptance, billing, and a valid claim?
- Who prepares, checks, numbers, prints, reprints, and assembles the packet?
- Which signing copy is authoritative, and how is an obsolete copy invalidated?
- Which TracePoint event proves custody and which separate action proves financial approval?

### Accounting and Treasury

- When are recognition and payment entries prepared, reviewed, posted, and reversed?
- How do subsidiary schedules and control accounts reconcile?
- What proves cash availability separately from budget availability?
- How do advice, release, remittance, bank acknowledgement, and bank reconciliation close the case?

## Close the session

- Read back the observed route, balance equations, signature/custody order, and exceptions.
- List every disagreement or missing artifact as a decision-log item with an owner and due date.
- Confirm what the participant demonstrated versus what they recalled or inferred.
- Assign evidence verification and actual-step review owners.
- Record the next office/role needed to continue the same authority and packet lineage.
- Do not mark the route accepted during the interview unless the named process owner has reviewed the resulting map and evidence.
